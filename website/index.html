Before you start

Three things to have ready

CerberusAI runs as a container in your own environment, and setup happens entirely in the browser.

Docker Required

The entire app ships as one container, so a single command replaces installing Python and its libraries by hand. Grab Docker Desktop — it's free — and you're ready.

A SIEM

Read-only access to your log platform — Wazuh, Elastic Security, or Security Onion. If you don't have one to test against, the bundle includes a throwaway lab to try it on.

An AI key

Your own model — Anthropic, OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. You keep control of the provider and the billing.

What it does

It investigates and decides — it doesn't just summarize the alert

Clears the noise

Auto-closes false alarms, with proof

Most alerts are benign, but a human still has to confirm each one. CerberusAI checks them itself and safely closes the routine ones, so your analysts never open them. That reclaimed time is the whole point.

Catches the pivot

Sees lateral movement by the map, not the log

It learns which machines normally talk to which. When one reaches a critical system it has never touched, that break in the pattern is escalated — often before any login has even succeeded.

Grounded

Hard evidence over hunches

Every verdict cites the queries it ran and a plain statistical score, like how many standard deviations a spike sits above normal. When the evidence is thin, it escalates rather than inventing a story.

Compounds

Sharper the longer it runs

A local memory starts empty and fills itself with your assets, baselines, and past decisions — tied to stable identities, so it keeps its footing through cloud and DHCP address churn.

No surprises

What it will and won't do

Strictly read-only

It reads from your SIEM and reasons about alerts. It never writes, blocks, quarantines, or reconfigures anything — not your hosts, not your network gear. There is deliberately no automation that touches your infrastructure.

Alert text goes to your chosen LLM

To investigate an alert, the relevant text is sent to the AI provider you configure, using your key. That is the only thing that leaves your environment — treat it like any AI tool your team adopts.

No telemetry, no phone-home

CerberusAI collects nothing and sends nothing back to the project. It's open source, so you can read exactly what it does.
